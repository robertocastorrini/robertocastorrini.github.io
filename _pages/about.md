I am an Assistant Professor in Mathematical Physics (RTD-A) at the [University of Tuscia](https://www.unitus.it/dipartimenti/deim/) and a visiting researcher at the [University of Pisa](https://www.dm.unipi.it/).

I study the **statistical properties of dynamical systems**, including complex systems, with a focus on systems that exhibit hyperbolic behaviour, using tools from Analysis and Probability.

## Research interests

- **Autonomous dynamical systems:** ergodicity, mixing, decay of correlations, thermodynamic formalism.
- **Complex systems:** convergence to equilibrium for mean-field-type systems.
- **Probability:** central limit and local limit theorems for deterministic "chaotic" systems.
- **Kinetic equations:** mean-field limits of Vlasov-type equations for discontinuous systems.
